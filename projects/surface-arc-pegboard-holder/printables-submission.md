## Description

Keep your Microsoft Arc Mouse within easy reach on your workshop or desk wall! This is a remix of the Lidl/Parkside Powerfix pegboard honeycomb holder, redesigned to mount directly into the pegboard's keyhole slots.

The mounting tab uses the same keyhole profile as the original Parkside hooks — just slide it in from above.

## Compatibility

Designed and tested for the newer Parkside/Powerfix pegboard with round keyholes. If you have the older version with square holes, you'll need to adapt the mounting tab.
