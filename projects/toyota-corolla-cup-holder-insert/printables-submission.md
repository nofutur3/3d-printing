## Description

The small front cup holder in a 2023 Corolla is deep enough that a small cup, like a coffee cup, sits low inside it and is annoying to fish back out. This two-part insert fixes that. A shell raises the floor of the cup holder, and a honeycomb lid sits on top so a small cup rests higher and is easy to grab.

If you need the full depth back for a tall cup or bottle, lift the lid out using the finger cutout in its rim.
