# Toyota Corolla (2023) cup holder insert

The small front cup holder in a 2023 Corolla is deep enough that a small cup (e.g. a coffee cup) sits low inside it and is awkward to pull back out. This two-part insert (shell + honeycomb lid) raises the floor of the cup holder so a small cup sits higher and is easy to grab. Pull the lid out and the cup holder is back to full depth for a tall cup or bottle.

## Instructions

Drop the shell into the cup holder well, then snap/rest the honeycomb lid on top. The cutout in the lid's rim is a finger-hold for lifting it back out. Remove the lid entirely when you need the cup holder's full depth.

Printed in PETG — recommended over PLA since the part sits in a car interior and can see high temperatures in direct sun.
